The Three Pillars

Sourcing, Roasting, Space

I

Sourcing (Terroir)

We travel to high-altitude micro-lots in Ethiopia, Costa Rica, and Brazil. Every bean is organically cultivated and ethically sourced, respecting the soil and the hands that harvest it.

II

Scandinavian Roast

Rejecting the bitter, over-roasted traditions of old Europe, we apply a gentle, light-to-medium Scandinavian profile. This coaxes out clean floral, fruit, and spice tasting notes hidden inside the bean.

III

Architectural Space

Designed by Scandinavian minimalists and executed in classic Parisian stone. Our spaces combine Honed Travertine, brushed copper, and soft linens to create a sensory sanctuary.

The Fusion

Parisian slow living meets Nord minimalism.

We believe that coffee is not just fuel; it is a ritual of pause. We combine the elegant elegance of a historic Parisian salon with the precise, hyper-focused quality control of Oslo.

The result is a delicate balance: a place that honors heritage while redefining what coffee can be. Welcome to the movement.
